# Excel-Sales-Dashboard---Bike-Sales-in-Europe
This is an Excel sales dashboard project that focuses on bike sales in Europe and looks at multiple factors that affect sales trends such as customer demographics, behavioral patterns, and sales distributions. 

**1. Project Overview**

The primary objective is to support strategic business decisions and data-driven decision-making by:

1. Analyzing Sales Trends: Identifying high-performing regions and demographics 

2. Customer Segmentation and Profiling: Looking at key demographics, segmenting customers by age group and customer gender to understand their behaviors

3. Sales Distributions: Examining the data and looking at trends and patterns in the total sales volumes by age group, country, category and sub-category

4. Data-Driven Insights: Proposing workable and actionable recommendations to optimize efficiency, increase sales, and enhance overall performance


**2. Datasets Summary**

The analysis is based on a bike sales dataset in Excel that contains historical transactions in Australia, Canada, France, Germany, UK, and the United States

**Key Variables:**

**Date, Month, Year:** Transaction dates,
**Customer Age, Age Group, Customer Gender:** Demographic details of the buyers,
**Country & State:** Geographic location of the sales, 
**Product Category, Sub-Category & Product:** Classifications of the items that were sold, 
**Order Quantity:** The number of units purchased,
**Unit Cost & Unit Price:** The pricing structure for the items that are being sold,
**Revenue, Cost, & Profit:** Key financial metrics that are derived for performance tracking


**3. Methodology**

**Data Preparation & Cleaning:**
- Used Excel functions to edit and filter raw data
- Handled missing values, removed duplicates, standardized formats and corrected data types to ensure data accuracy
- Created a Working Sheet section to conduct the analysis and preserve the raw data in the original sheet

**Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** 
- Built and used Pivot Tables for creating data visualizations
- Visualized relationships between different products, categories, sub-categories, countries, and customer demographics

**Interactive Dashboarding:** 
- Built a comprehensive Excel sales dashboard to provide a panoramic view of the business's performance
- Created and put in dynamic filters (slicers) in the Year, Month, Age Group, Country, Customer Gender and Product categories


**4. Dashboard Proposal**

**Overall Performance:** Total revenue is $84 million and the total profit is $32 million with an overall profit margin of 37%.

**Category Mix (Revenue & Profit):** Bikes are the dominant category in revenue and profits followed by Accessories and Clothing. 

**Top Products by Revenue:** Road-150 Red bikes are the top selling product ($3.8M) followed by Mountain-200 Black ($3.3M) and Mountain-200 Silver ($3.0M)

**Key Insights:** Identified Age Group, Country, Customer Gender and Product Category as the strongest predictors of customer bike purchases. Customers who were in the Youth and Seniors categories more likely to purchase a bike compared to Adults and Young Adults. 

**Customer Segmentation:** Revenue is nearly split even by Customer Gender (Male 52% vs Female 48%), By age group, Seniors are the top revenue segment followed by Youth and Adults. 

**Top Markets & Profit Margin:** The United States, Australia, and the UK lead in overall sales and profits, whereas Canada, France, and Germany have sizeable markets among certain customer demographics. Canada has the highest profit margin (mid to upper 40% range) followed by the UK (lower 40% range).
